# Institutional archival search — 27 October 2026

Research candidates and rejected leads, not a list of production-cleared files. See `institutional-candidates.json` for individual records.

The German Federal Archive released authentic images of Iraqi students abroad under CC BY-SA 3.0 Germany through its Wikimedia cooperation. They can illustrate Iraqi people studying only with captions explicitly stating the GDR location and date. They cannot establish Iraqi school facilities, the reach of free education, or Ba'athist-era outcomes (the 1960 images predate the 1968 government).

National Library of Israel / Europeana supplies three authentic 1976 Yosi Rot photographs called *Visiting the Kurds in Iraq* under CC BY 4.0. Visual inspection shows a group of women and children carrying containers by tents, a family portrait, and a group of men. The source does not identify educational, industrial, medical, or prosperity-related activity. Do not relabel these as school, healthcare or oil scenes. No production copies acquired.

No institutionally licensed 1960–1989 Iraqi oil-field, industrial-development or hospital image was cleared in this search. This is a sourcing gap, not evidence that such images do not exist.

Rejected or unlicensed leads:

- [University of Baghdad rare-media gallery](https://en.uobaghdad.edu.iq/?p=227): historical source lead, but no reuse license found.
- [University of Baghdad students, 1978/79, Bahira Abdulatif](https://museoecologiahumana.org/en/obras/estudiantes-del-departamento-de-lengua-espanola-en-la-universidad-de-bagdad/): photograph credited © Bahira Abdulatif; no open license confirmed.
- [Rumaila inauguration, 1972, Edith Maubec, Exeter University](https://humanities-collections.exeter.ac.uk/dame/s/en/item/1268): excellent historical match; no reuse permission confirmed from the accessible item metadata.
- [YIT construction projects in Iraq, Central Archives for Finnish Business Records / Finna](https://www.finna.fi/Record/elka.143412884804100_153475972730700?filter%5B%5D=geographic_facet%3A%22Irak%22&hiddenFilters%5B%5D=%21id%3A%22elka.143412884804100%22&hiddenFilters%5B%5D=hierarchy_top_id%3A%22elka.143412884804100%22&sid=3459329254&type=AllFields): authentic 1981 Baghdad Island construction and 1984 infrastructure photos, but institution states usage license unspecified / copyright not evaluated. Excluded.
- Commons *Some Kurdish Medical Doctors in the 1970s, Sulaymani, Iraqi Kurdistan.webp*: uploader applied CC0 to a photograph by an unknown photographer taken from Facebook. A rights-holder release was not established. Excluded.
- Commons *IPC oil derrick.jpg*: metadata lists an upload-era date of 2011, but original Matson collection photograph is from about 1932. Excluded from the requested 1960–1989 range.
- ETH-Bibliothek Iraq/Baghdad searches located Walter Mittelholzer photographs from 1925, outside the requested date range; modern Comet result is an aircraft at Zurich, not Iraqi development.
- Internet Archive search located *Baghdad 83* (1983, Emat Bahjat) with an uploader-applied Public Domain Mark. That mark alone does not establish rights-holder permission. No production download.

Firecrawl search was unavailable because its connected account had no credits; no retry was made. Native web search, Commons metadata API, Internet Archive metadata/search API, Europeana metadata API and institution pages were used instead. Finna API requests returned HTTP 403; indexed institutional page text remained accessible through web search.
